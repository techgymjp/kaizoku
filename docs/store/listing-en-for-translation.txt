[Full description] (max 4000 characters)
You are Minamoto no Yoshitsune. You are the captain of a pirate ship.
You start at Himeji port. You sail west across the Seto Inland Sea.
The sea and more than 140 islands are made in 3D from real map data and real aerial photos.
Land on the islands and take their treasure. Sink the enemy ships. Your goal is Ganryūjima, where the last battle takes place.

■ BATTLES AT SEA
• Five gunners stand at the front of your ship. They shoot in the direction your ship faces.
• Shots hit more often when the enemy is close.
• Your cannon aims at the enemy in front of your ship and hits many enemies at once.
• Each enemy ship has 8 crew. When all of them fall, the ship explodes.
• Large enemy ships also fire cannons. Before they fire, a red ring appears on the sea. Turn your ship to avoid it.
• The wind blows west. The tide changes during the day. There are whirlpools.

■ BATTLES ON THE ISLANDS
• A golden light marks each port. Sail into it to land.
• Use the sword and the spear. Hold the "Full" button to run fast.
• Each island has real hills, towns, fields, orange trees, rice fields and shrines.
• More than 800 famous warriors from Japanese history fight you. The farther west you go, the newer their era.
• A warrior you defeat does not come back.

■ VISIT MORE ISLANDS FOR AN EASIER LAST BATTLE
• Each treasure makes your ship stronger. Your maximum HP goes up.
• Warriors from islands you did not visit come to help the enemy in the last battle.
• Ships you did not sink also come to Ganryūjima. Sink all of them, then reach the island.

■ OTHER FEATURES
• Fireworks when you take a treasure and when you win.
• Exciting pirate music.
• The game saves when you go back to the title screen. Press "Continue" to play again from there.
• If you fall, you can watch an ad to continue with full HP. You can do this 3 times in one game.
• Share your results with friends.
• Japanese and English.
• The game needs an internet connection to load the islands.

Map data and aerial photos: Geospatial Information Authority of Japan (GSI Tiles)
